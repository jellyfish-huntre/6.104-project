Roam visual study · Buildable map samples

What these would actually look like

These four maps are rendered entirely with HTML and CSS. They use Roam’s current 10×10 movement grid, four open booth footprints, furniture, collision positions, and a walking avatar. Open one to move around with WASD or arrow keys. The scenes use no generated images.

01 · Pixel Fair

Sprite-like

Square furniture and rugs, tiled hall floor, and a small blocky avatar. A feasible first step toward the Gather feeling.

Best for: a real world to explore. Requires matching sprite assets to go further.

02 · Playful Flat

Simple + social

Rounded booth areas and furniture on a navy floor. Closest to Roam’s landing screen and the uncluttered Gimkit direction.

Best for: bold, easy to read booths and a small implementation scope.

03 · Night Arcade

Dark + glowing

Glowing booth outlines and darker floor tiles give each area a stronger game feel while keeping the same top-down map.

Best for: a bolder identity. Needs careful contrast in the real UI.

04 · Indoor Gallery

Warm + quiet

Wood-toned furniture, a light textured floor, and soft green booth rugs make the hall feel indoorsy without buildings.

Best for: a calm fair. Less connected to Roam’s dark playful UI.

Implementation scope: this is a small coded prototype of the map layer, not a screenshot or polished asset pack. The live app is unchanged. Tables and plants in the playable previews have blocked grid cells; the red overlay lets you inspect them.
