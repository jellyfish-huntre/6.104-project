# Beta user journey

Adam has half an hour to explore an online fair. At a typical video fair, he would have to open meeting links one by one to find out who is available, then stay on a call while he waits for a chance to ask a question. He wants to see his options and keep moving.

The organizer creates a fair in Roam, adds booths, and shares its code. Adam enters that code without making an account. He arrives in the fair hall as an avatar and walks toward a booth that catches his attention. He selects its area to read the description and see what is happening there.

The representative has opened an **open group** call. Adam walks into the booth and chooses **Open video call**. Roam opens the booth's external meeting in a new tab, where Adam listens to the conversation and asks about upcoming events. He returns to the fair map afterward and continues exploring. [Figure 1 shows the open group booth state](p3-evidence/open-group.png).

Later, the representative closes the group call and opens an **invite-only** call at the booth. It is one meeting for that booth, but only people with an active invitation can get its link through Roam. Adam chooses **Request a turn**, then walks to another part of the fair. His waiting request stays visible while he explores. He can leave the queue if he changes his mind.

The representative can see the waiting order and chooses **Send invitation** for the next visitor. Adam sees a two-minute countdown and chooses **Open video call** before it expires. The invitation is still there if he reloads the page. Roam checks his invitation before opening the booth's meeting link; another visitor cannot use Adam's request to get it. [Figure 2 shows Adam's invitation](p3-evidence/invite-only.png), and [Figure 3 shows the representative's queue controls](p3-evidence/mobile-host.png).

If the representative stops new requests, people already waiting keep their places, while a new visitor sees that the booth is not taking requests. If an invitation expires, Adam sees that it expired and can no longer open the call through Roam. Roam manages access to the link, but the conversation itself happens in the external video meeting.
