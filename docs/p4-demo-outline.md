# P4 demo outline (under three minutes)

Record the public app at [roam.mit-sdg.dev](https://roam.mit-sdg.dev/) after the final commit is live. Narrate throughout. Use two browser windows: an organizer account and a guest. Keep a meeting page ready, but the point of the demo is Roam's access and queue flow.

| Time | Show | Narration cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Public landing page and the demo fair; enter its code as a guest. | “I made Roam for the conversations you do not know to schedule. A visitor can enter a fair without an account, see what is here, and decide where to go.” |
| 0:25–0:55 | Walk to a booth, open its description and directory, then show the minimap. | “The hall is shared and grows as booths are added. The directory helps me find a booth, and the map still feels like a place I can explore.” |
| 0:55–1:25 | As organizer, add a booth by choosing a size, show its automatic placement, then move it. | “The organizer describes the booth and picks a size. Roam finds a spaced position; I can adjust it afterward.” |
| 1:25–1:50 | Open a group call as representative; guest enters the booth and opens its meeting. | “For an open conversation, the representative starts the call and someone at the booth can open its link.” |
| 1:50–2:30 | Switch to invite-only, guest requests a turn and walks elsewhere, representative invites them, guest opens the link. | “When a representative is busy, I can request a turn without waiting in an empty meeting. The invitation gives access to the current private call for two minutes.” |
| 2:30–2:50 | Close or switch the private call, then show the old invitation cannot open its link. | “The server checks the live call as well as the invitation. A stale invitation does not reveal an old private link.” |
| 2:50–3:00 | End on fair map and point to README/test record. | “The repository has the concept specs, final design, and reproducible tests, including the deployed checks.” |

Before recording, verify the demo account, booth, and meeting links are ready. The public fair should have at least one described booth. Keep the cursor and browser zoom steady. Cut pauses between actions while preserving narration across the full video. After uploading, add the video link to the README and check that the link opens without signing in.
